Reserve funds now, capture or release later. Best for bookings/orders where the final amount may change.
Authorisation (Command: Authorisation)
Store returned TransactionIndex
Increase amount — Additional Authorisation (optional)
Capture — Debit with TransactionIndex
OR Cancel — AuthorisationReversal with TransactionIndex

Prepayment Flow (Immediate Debit)
Take payment immediately. Customer pays upfront before service/booking is confirmed.
Immediate payment — Command: Debit with PAN
Store full payment response & TransactionIndex
Mark order as paid only if Status = 0 (Approved)
Refund later — Credit with TransactionIndex

ℹ️ iVeri Enterprise Guide (Section: Actions): Authorisation (also known as "Preauthorisation") reserves funds. Debit (also known as "Sale" or "Purchase") transfers funds immediately. Credit (also known as "Refund" or "Pay") returns funds to the cardholder.
